LocoNotas is the lab notebook. It holds the dated material that does not belong in a project's reference docs: reading notes, experiment logs, hardware quirks, and ideas that are not yet a finding.

The rule is one home per artifact. Stable documentation lives on the project sites. Formal papers and grant outputs live on [research.borck.dev](https://research.borck.dev). Dated observations live here.

## What a note records

- The date and the context
- The source, if there is one
- What was observed
- What is still uncertain
- The project or projects it touches

## Status labels

- `draft`: not ready to read
- `published`: written, not necessarily verified
- `unverified`: a claim from an external source that we have not checked
- `verified`: checked against a primary source or a measurement
- `superseded`: replaced by a newer note

The first notes will cover the [LocoFósil](https://locofosil.org) hardware shelf and the Optiplex plus K1200 build.
