These are the books and papers behind the lab's position on thinking with AI. Links go to the author's sites. Notes on individual chapters will appear as separate entries.

## Books

### Conversation, Not Delegation: How to Think With AI, Not Just Use It

The method underneath the lab's thesis: brainstorm, ideate, iterate, amplify. Delegation outsources judgement; conversation keeps it in the practitioner.

[Read the book](https://michael-borck.github.io/conversation-not-delegation) · [Framework site](https://conversationnotdelegation.com/)

### The Human Edge: Using AI with Judgement

The workplace companion. Judge, brief, check, deliver. Written for people who have to sign their name to the output.

[Read the book](https://thehumanedge.borck.dev/)

### Partner, Don't Police: AI in the Business Classroom

A companion for educators working out what AI should do and what students must still own.

[Read the book](https://michael-borck.github.io/partner-dont-police)

### Substantiate, Don't Assume: Why the Auditor's Opinion Matters More Than Ever in the Age of AI

A companion for audit and assurance work, where the judgement behind the output is the product.

[Read the book](https://michael-borck.github.io/substantiate-dont-assume)

All books are free to download at [books.borck.education](https://books.borck.education).

## How these connect to the lab

The lab's thesis is that local AI should amplify thinking, not replace it. The books are the long version of that argument. LocoMusa measures thinking-partner quality, and LocoAgente builds the harness that keeps the human in the loop. The reading list is the conceptual layer under both.
